# Florin-Stefan Morar

**Historian · National University of Singapore**

I am Assistant Professor of History and Director of the History+AI Lab at NUS. My research connects global history, the history of science, and computational humanities. I study how knowledge travels across cultures and changes through technology.

I received my PhD in the History of Science from Harvard University in 2019.

### Selected research

- **Science and Emotions:** Exploring the emotional dimensions of scientific life through historical research and computational humanities.
- **The Pulse of History:** Investigating how historical scholarship changes over time through computational analysis and historical interpretation.
- **Connected Cartographies:** My book examines exchanges between Chinese and European traditions of geography and cartography. [Cambridge University Press](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009636049).
